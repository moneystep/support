Identity documents, selfies, and any biometric data are collected and held by the provider, not the Platform; the Platform stores only the verification result
  • Biometric information. To verify your age and identity before your first withdrawal, our identity verification provider compares a selfie you take with the photo on your government-issued ID. That comparison may create a scan of face geometry, which is a biometric identifier under the laws of some states, including Illinois and Texas. We and our provider collect it only to verify your identity and age and to prevent fraud. Before the process begins, you will be shown these biometric terms and asked for your written (electronic) consent; you cannot complete verification, and therefore cannot withdraw, without it. Biometric identifiers are never sold, leased, traded, or otherwise profited from, are never used for any purpose other than verification, and are disclosed only to the provider that performs it, or where required by law or a valid subpoena or warrant. Retention and destruction: the provider retains biometric identifiers only for the period stated in its privacy notice, which is linked on the verification screen, and in no case longer than three (3) years after verification is performed, and then permanently destroys them. The provider stores biometric data using the same or a greater standard of care than it applies to other confidential information, and at least the reasonable standard of care in its industry.
  • Financial information: transaction history, entry fees paid, winnings received, and wallet balance
  • Step count data: daily step totals sourced from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect
  • Sync status data: whether background access to your step data is enabled for the App on your device (where your device reports this), and the date and time your step data was last synced to the Platform
  • Location and eligibility data: our third-party location verification provider checks your location at account creation, at each entry into a paid or cash-prize Challenge, and at each withdrawal request, solely to determine the U.S. state you are in for eligibility verification. Your GPS coordinates are resolved to a U.S. state and then discarded. For each check we retain the resulting two-letter U.S. state, the time of the check, what triggered it, whether it passed, the IP address and IP-based location used for the check, your device’s region setting, and any VPN or location-spoofing signal. We do not store your coordinates and we do not track your movements
  • Device information: device token for push notifications, operating system, and app version
  • Usage data: Challenge participation history, competition results, and in-app activity
  • Payment and withdrawal information: the payment method you use to pay an entry fee and receive a payout is collected and stored by our licensed third-party payment processor, not by the Platform. We retain the record of amounts, dates, and status
  • Tax information: when your cumulative winnings reach the reporting threshold described in the Terms of Service, we request the Social Security Number (SSN) or Employer Identification Number (EIN) required to issue a Form 1099 and process further payouts
  • Fraud-prevention data: records relating to suspicious, irregular, or anomalous activity, including flagged step totals and related details, retained to protect the integrity of competitions
  • Website information: if you submit your email address on the Website (for example, to join our mailing list), we collect it together with any other details you choose to enter, such as your state. 2. Health & Step Data

The App accesses your step count data from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android) for the purposes of tracking your performance in Challenges, calculating rank tier, and detecting manipulation of step data.

Step data retention: We store your detailed daily step data for up to 90 days to support Challenges of varying lengths. Daily step data older than 90 days is automatically deleted from our servers on a rolling basis. We access historical step data from your health app to calculate your rank tier but do not store the underlying records beyond this window. Aggregated or derived figures — such as your rolling step average, cumulative Challenge step totals, and your final step count in a completed Challenge — are kept as part of your profile and competition records and may be retained longer.

We do not use your health data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with any entity outside of the Platform for any purpose other than operating the App.

Background step sync: With your permission, the App also reads your step count data from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect periodically while the App is in the background or closed, and syncs it to the Platform, so that Challenge leaderboards stay reasonably current for all entrants. Granting background access to your step data is a condition of enrolling in Challenges. Background syncing is performed on a best-efforts basis and is controlled by your device’s operating system; it is not guaranteed to run at any particular time or frequency.

You may revoke HealthKit or Health Connect permissions, including background access, at any time through your device settings. Revoking access will prevent step tracking from functioning, may prevent you from enrolling in Challenges, and may cause your leaderboard entry to show as not recently synced.

5. Entry Fees and Payment

How payment works: Entry fees paid by card or another external payment method are collected through a licensed third-party payment processor. When you enroll in a paid Challenge, the processor places an authorization on your payment method, and funds are captured when the Challenge starts. If the Challenge does not proceed, any authorization is released and any entry fee already captured is refunded in full. An entrant whose fee cannot be captured is not counted toward the Challenge’s minimum number of entrants. An entry fee paid from your wallet balance is deducted from your wallet when you enroll and restored to your wallet if the Challenge does not proceed.

Entry fees: Each paid Challenge has a flat entry fee, stated in full on the Challenge card before you enter and fixed once posted. The entry fee is the price of entry to the Challenge. It is not a contribution to a shared fund, and no part of it is described or calculated as a percentage of any prize. Entry fees do not exceed $40 per Challenge.

Card processing fee: If you pay an entry fee by card or another external payment method, a card processing fee is added that passes through what our payment processor charges for the transaction, with no markup. The entry fee, the card processing fee, and the total are each shown to you before you confirm payment, and the total is the amount authorized on your payment method and captured when the Challenge starts. The card processing fee is separate from the entry fee, funds no prize, and is not calculated by reference to any prize. No card processing fee applies to an entry fee paid from your wallet balance or to a Free Entry. The card processing fee is not part of the entry fee and does not count toward the $40 entry-fee limit. Wherever these Terms provide that an authorization is released or an entry fee is refunded, the card processing fee is released or refunded with it; wherever an entry fee is non-refundable, the card processing fee is non-refundable too.

Free Challenges: Some Challenges have no entry fee and still award cash prizes. Free Challenges are governed by these Terms in the same way as paid Challenges, except that the entry-fee, payment, and refund provisions do not apply. No prize in a free Challenge is allocated by chance.

Bank fees and chargebacks: If a charge results in an overdraft or other fee from your bank, that fee is your responsibility. If you dispute or reverse a captured entry fee with your card issuer or bank, the Platform may remove you from the Challenge, withhold any prize for it, and hold your wallet balance until the dispute is resolved. If the dispute is resolved against you, we may deduct the disputed amount and any associated fees from your wallet balance.

6. Prizes and the Prize Guarantee

Prizes: For each paid Challenge, the paid places and the amount awarded for each place are set by the Platform or by a sponsor and posted on the Challenge card before enrollment opens. Prize amounts do not vary with the number of entrants or with the amount of entry fees collected. Prizes are funded from a segregated prize reserve, which may be a single account used across Challenges with each Challenge’s prizes recorded separately, funded before enrollment opens in the amount of the posted prizes. Once a Challenge proceeds, the posted prizes are paid in full whether or not entry fees cover them. The only conditions on payment of a posted prize are that the Challenge proceeds and that an eligible entrant finishes in that place.

The prize guarantee and its single condition: Every Challenge states a minimum number of entrants before enrollment opens. That minimum is the only condition on the Challenge proceeding. The minimum is measured when the Challenge starts, after entry fees are captured; an entrant whose entry fee cannot be captured is not counted. If fewer than the minimum number of entrants remain enrolled at that point, the Challenge does not proceed: it is cancelled automatically, any entry fee already captured is refunded in full, remaining authorizations are released, no result is recorded, and no prize is awarded. If enrollment closes with fewer than the minimum number of entrants, the Challenge is cancelled at that point and no entry fee is captured.

If the minimum is met, the Challenge proceeds and the posted prizes become an obligation of the Platform, conditional only on an eligible entrant finishing in each paid place. They are paid in full, exactly as posted, regardless of how many entrants above the minimum enroll, regardless of how much is collected in entry fees, and regardless of whether entry fees cover the prizes. The Platform bears any shortfall. No prize amount is reduced, withheld, or recalculated because enrollment was lower than expected. If fewer eligible entrants finish than there are paid places, the unfilled places are not paid.

Ties: If two or more entrants tie for a cash-winning placement, they split the combined payouts of the placements occupied by the tied entrants. For example, if three entrants tie for 4th place in a Challenge with ten cash-winning places, those three split the combined 4th, 5th, and 6th place payouts, and the entrant with the next-highest step count receives the 7th place payout. If entrants tie for the final cash-winning place, they split that single payout. Because ties can affect the amount an individual entrant receives, posted per-place amounts are shown as expected amounts until the Challenge is settled.

8. Exiting a Challenge

You may voluntarily exit a Challenge at any time before or after it begins. For a short period while a Challenge is starting and entry fees are being captured, exits are temporarily unavailable; you may exit again once the Challenge has started. The following rules apply:

Before the Challenge starts: You will be removed from the Challenge, your entry-fee authorization is released (or, if you paid from your wallet, the fee is restored to your wallet, or, if you used a Free Entry, it is restored), and you are not charged. You are free to join another Challenge immediately.

After the Challenge starts: Your entry fee is non-refundable, and a Free Entry applied to that Challenge is not restored. You will be removed from the Challenge and leaderboard. You are free to join another Challenge immediately after exiting.

Exits are recorded in your career statistics. Exiting mid-Challenge counts as a last-place finish for statistical purposes. Steps you took during an exited Challenge are not credited toward your career step totals or any global leaderboard.

12. Step Data Sync Requirements

To ensure accurate leaderboard standings and final results, users are responsible for the following:

  • A connected step source is required. The App reads your step data from Apple Health (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android). You are responsible for ensuring a step source is actively recording steps into your health app and that the App has been granted permission to read it. On Android in particular, Health Connect does not record steps on its own — it only stores step data provided by another app or a connected wearable. If no step source is feeding your health app, or if permission is not granted, your steps will register as zero regardless of your physical activity. It is your sole responsibility to confirm your steps are being recorded before and during a Challenge.
  • Background sync permission is required to enroll. To enroll in a Challenge, you must grant the App permission to read your step data while the App is in the background or closed, when prompted during enrollment (on iOS, through the Apple Health permissions screen; on Android, through the Health Connect background-access permission). This allows the App to keep your counted step total reasonably current on the Challenge leaderboard without requiring you to open the App. Where your device reports the status of this permission, the App may decline enrollment while it is not granted. You agree to keep this permission enabled for the duration of any Challenge you enter and its post-Challenge sync window. Background syncing is best-efforts and controlled by your device’s operating system; it supplements, and does not replace, your responsibilities under this Section. Sync recency is visible to other entrants. The time your steps were last synced to the Platform is displayed with your entry on the live leaderboard of any Challenge you are enrolled in.
  • A single step source is used, and only that source counts. Before enrolling, one step source is set for your account — Apple Health by default on iOS, and Garmin on Android, which requires a connected Garmin device — and you may change it in Settings at any time before you enroll. Only steps recorded by that source will be counted toward your Challenge results; steps from any other app, device, or source will not be included. The source is locked to your entry at the time you enroll and cannot be changed for the duration of that Challenge. It is your responsibility to choose the source that accurately reflects the steps you physically take and to keep that source recording throughout the Challenge. If your selected source stops recording or syncing, your step total may stop advancing, and the most recently synced total will be used. Manually entered steps are never counted, regardless of source. On iOS, eligible sources are Apple Health (covering your iPhone’s built-in step counter and Apple Watch), Oura, and Garmin. On Android, Garmin is currently the only eligible source — if you use an Android device without a connected Garmin, no eligible step source is available to you and you will not be able to accumulate countable steps. Eligible sources may change as devices and platforms change.
  • Keep your apps synced during a Challenge. During an active Challenge, make sure your steps and any connected health or fitness apps (such as your wearable’s companion app, for example Oura or Garmin Connect) are synced and up to date, and open the App regularly during the Challenge. The App reads your step data from Apple Health or Google Health Connect when you open the App, and those services only contain your latest steps if your phone and any connected apps have synced.
  • Post-Challenge sync window. After a Challenge has ended, make sure your steps and any connected health or fitness apps are synced and up to date, and open the App within 24 hours of the Challenge end time, so your complete step data is captured before results are finalized.
  • Settlement timing. Challenge results are finalized approximately 24 hours after the Challenge end time. Step data not synced by settlement will not be included in your final result.
  • Incomplete sync liability. If you fail to sync within the 24-hour post-Challenge window, your recorded step count at the time of settlement will be used as your final total. The Platform is not responsible for any difference between your actual steps and your synced step count.
  • Syncing depends on your apps and on opening our App. The App syncs your steps when you open it, and it can only read steps that your phone and connected apps have already synced into Apple Health or Google Health Connect. When you have granted background access, the App attempts to sync your steps periodically while it is closed or in the background, but background syncing is controlled by your device’s operating system, is not guaranteed to run at any particular time or frequency, and may be prevented by device settings, battery optimization, or platform restrictions. You should not rely on background syncing alone. Keeping your health and fitness apps synced, and opening the App to sync — including within the post-Challenge window described above — remains your responsibility.
  • Android users: refresh Garmin Connect at the end of each day. On Android, Moneystep reads your steps from Google Health Connect, and Health Connect holds only what the Garmin Connect app has written into it. Steps that remain on your watch and have not synced to Garmin Connect do not exist as far as the App is concerned. Refresh Garmin Connect at the end of each day, after you have taken your steps for that day. Refreshing earlier in the day does not reliably write that day’s full step count into Health Connect, and steps taken after an early refresh may not be written for that day at all. This is a limitation of how Garmin Connect and Health Connect exchange data and is outside the Platform’s control. We also recommend opening Moneystep once a day so you can confirm your total is advancing as expected. If your recorded total is lower than the steps you physically took because your source did not sync, the Platform is not responsible for the difference.
  • Android battery optimization. Some Android device manufacturers (including but not limited to Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, and OnePlus) apply aggressive battery-saving features that can prevent the App from receiving notifications and can interfere with syncing between Garmin Connect, Health Connect, and the App. If you use one of these devices, we recommend exempting both Moneystep and Garmin Connect from battery optimization in your device settings. You can typically find this under Battery → App optimization or Battery → Background usage limits. The Platform is not responsible for missed notifications or incomplete step data resulting from manufacturer battery-optimization settings.

If you do not open the App at all during a Challenge and also do not open it within 24 hours after the Challenge ends, no step data can be captured for that Challenge. In that case your steps cannot be recorded and your final total for the Challenge will be zero (0), regardless of how many steps you physically took.
