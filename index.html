How We Handle Your Data

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use the Platform, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: username, email address, phone number, and profile photo
  • Consent records: a record that you confirmed you are 18 years of age or older and accepted these Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, together with the date and time of that confirmation
  • Age and identity verification information: your date of birth, and the result returned by our third-party identity verification provider. Identity documents you submit are collected and held by that provider; the Platform does not store copies of them
  • Financial information: transaction history, entry fees paid, winnings received, and wallet balance
  • Step count data: daily step totals sourced from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect
  • Sync status data: whether background access to your step data is enabled for the App on your device (where your device reports this), and the date and time your step data was last synced to the Platform
  • Location and eligibility data: our third-party location verification provider checks your location at account creation, at each paid challenge entry, and at each withdrawal request, solely to determine the U.S. state you are in for eligibility verification. Your GPS coordinates are resolved to a U.S. state and then discarded. We retain only the resulting two-letter U.S. state, the time of the check, what triggered it, whether it passed, and any VPN or location-spoofing signal reported by our verification provider. We do not store your coordinates and we do not track your movements
  • Device information: device token for push notifications, operating system, and app version
  • Usage data: challenge participation history, competition results, and in-app activity
  • Payment and withdrawal information: the payment method you use to pay an entry fee and receive a payout is collected and stored by our licensed third-party payment processor, not by the Platform. We retain the record of amounts, dates, and status
  • Tax information: when your cumulative winnings reach the reporting threshold described in the Terms of Service, we collect the Social Security Number (SSN) or Employer Identification Number (EIN) required to issue a Form 1099 and process further payouts
  • Fraud-prevention data: records relating to suspicious, irregular, or anomalous activity, including flagged step totals and related details, retained to protect the integrity of competitions

How we use this information: We use the information above only to operate the Platform — to create and secure your account; to run challenges and maintain accurate live leaderboards, standings, and results; to verify your age, identity, and location where required; to collect entry fees and pay prizes through our payment processor; to detect and prevent fraud and protect the integrity of competitions; to meet our tax, legal, and regulatory obligations; to provide support and send you transactional notifications; and to maintain the security of the App. We collect each category above for one or more of these purposes and do not use it for unrelated purposes. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it (see Section 4).

2. Health & Step Data

The App accesses your step count data from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android) for the purposes of tracking your performance in challenges, calculating rank tier, and detecting manipulation of step data.

Step data retention: We store your detailed daily step data for up to 90 days to support challenges of varying lengths. Daily step data older than 90 days is automatically deleted from our servers on a rolling basis. We access historical step data from your health app to calculate your rank tier but do not store the underlying records beyond this window. Aggregated or derived figures — such as your rolling step average, cumulative challenge step totals, and your final step count in a completed challenge — are kept as part of your profile and competition records and may be retained longer.

We do not use your health data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with any entity outside of the Platform for any purpose other than operating the App.

Background step sync: With your permission, the App also reads your step count data from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect periodically while the App is in the background or closed, and syncs it to the Platform, so that challenge leaderboards stay reasonably current for all entrants. Granting background access to your step data is a condition of enrolling in challenges. Background syncing is performed on a best-efforts basis and is controlled by your device's operating system; it is not guaranteed to run at any particular time or frequency.

You may revoke HealthKit or Health Connect permissions, including background access, at any time through your device settings. Revoking access will prevent step tracking from functioning, may prevent you from enrolling in challenges, and may cause your leaderboard entry to show as not recently synced.

3. Third-Party Services

The Platform uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Google Firebase — backend infrastructure used for our database (Cloud Firestore), user authentication (Firebase Authentication), server logic (Cloud Functions), file storage (Cloud Storage, e.g. profile photos), and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Data is stored on Firebase servers subject to Google's privacy policies.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability diagnostics. When the App crashes, Crashlytics collects diagnostic information including your device model, operating system version, and app version to help us identify and fix problems. This data is processed by Google.
  • App Check (Apple App Attest / Google Play Integrity) — device attestation used to verify that requests to our backend come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App, to protect against abuse and fraud. This involves device-integrity tokens processed by Apple and Google.
  • Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect — read-only access to step count data on your device.
  • Payment processor — a licensed third-party payment processor collects entry fees and disburses prize payments. Your payment method details are provided to and held by that processor.
  • Location verification provider — resolves your device location to a U.S. state for eligibility and detects VPN use and location spoofing.
  • Identity and age verification provider — verifies your identity and age before your first withdrawal.
  • Email delivery — we use a Google Workspace email service to send you transactional messages (such as account notices and withdrawal confirmations). Delivering these emails involves processing your email address.

We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs that track behavior across apps, or any other third-party data brokers.

Backups are maintained solely for disaster-recovery and data-integrity purposes and are not used to reconstitute deleted accounts.

The Platform operates skill-based endurance competitions. Each challenge is decided by the entrant's own total step count over the challenge period. Challenge outcomes are determined entirely by step count performance, which reflects physical effort, fitness level, and activity — and not by chance, luck, or any random element.

The Platform is not a game of chance and is not gambling. Winning depends solely on the number of steps a participant physically takes, which is an action wholly within each participant's own control and influence. The Platform does not reward participants for any outcome determined by chance. No prize, placement, or tiebreak is allocated by chance. Any attempt to win based on anything other than steps you physically take — including any manipulation of step or health data — is strictly prohibited and is grounds for disqualification and forfeiture.

By participating, you acknowledge that results are based on skill and physical effort, that no outcome is guaranteed, and that the Platform makes no representations regarding your likelihood of winning any challenge.

5. Entry Fees, Prizes, Payment, and Refunds

How payment works: Entry fees are collected through a licensed third-party payment processor. When you enroll in a paid challenge, the processor places an authorization on your payment method. Funds are captured when the challenge starts. If the challenge does not start, the authorization is released and you are not charged.

Entry fees: Each paid challenge has a flat entry fee, stated in full on the challenge card before you enter and fixed once posted. The entry fee is the price of entry to the challenge. It is not a contribution to a shared fund, and no part of it is described or calculated as a percentage of any prize. Entry fees do not exceed $40 per challenge.

Prizes: For each paid challenge, the paid places and the amount awarded for each place are set by the Platform or by a sponsor and posted on the challenge card before the signup period opens. Prize amounts do not vary with the number of entrants or with the amount of entry fees collected. Prizes are funded from a segregated prize reserve funded before the signup period opens. Once a challenge proceeds, the posted prizes are paid in full whether or not entry fees cover them. The only condition on payment of a posted prize is that the challenge proceeds.

Minimum entrant count: Every Challenge states a minimum number of entrants before enrollment opens. That minimum is the only condition on the prize. If the minimum is not met by the enrollment deadline, the Challenge does not proceed: it is cancelled automatically, no funds are captured, every entry fee already collected is refunded in full, no result is recorded, and no prize is awarded.

If the minimum is met, the Challenge proceeds and the posted prizes become an unconditional obligation of the Platform. They are paid in full, exactly as posted, regardless of how many entrants above the minimum enroll, regardless of how much is collected in entry fees, and regardless of whether entry fees cover the prizes. The Platform bears any shortfall. No prize amount is reduced, withheld, or recalculated because enrollment was lower than expected.

Cancellation before a challenge starts: The Platform may cancel a challenge at any time before it starts, for failure to meet the minimum entrant count or for any other reason. On cancellation, every authorization is released and every entry fee already collected is refunded in full. A Free Entry applied to a cancelled challenge is restored.

Cancellation after a challenge starts: The Platform may cancel a challenge after it has begun only where the challenge cannot be completed fairly or lawfully, including a technical failure affecting the integrity of scoring, a legal or regulatory requirement, or an event described in Section 23. In that case no results are recorded for that challenge, no prizes are awarded, and every entry fee for that challenge is refunded in full. A Free Entry applied to a cancelled challenge is restored.

No other refunds: Except as described above, all entry fees are final and non-refundable once a challenge has started. An entry fee will not be returned for voluntary exit, injury, illness, device malfunction, loss of connectivity, step sync failure, removal for prohibited conduct, or dissatisfaction with results.

Ties: If two or more entrants tie for a cash-winning placement, they split the combined payouts of the placements occupied by the tied entrants. For example, if three entrants tie for 4th place in a challenge with ten cash-winning places, those three split the combined 4th, 5th, and 6th place payouts, and the entrant with the next-highest step count receives the 7th place payout. If entrants tie for the final cash-winning place, they split that single payout. Because ties can affect the amount an individual entrant receives, posted per-place amounts are shown as expected amounts until the challenge is settled.

Payouts: Prizes are credited to your in-app wallet when the challenge is settled and are paid through our licensed third-party payment processor.

Your in-app wallet: Your wallet holds only prize winnings. You may not deposit funds into your wallet, and the Platform does not issue credits, bonuses, or promotional balances to it. Wallet funds may not be transferred to another user. You may withdraw your balance on demand at any time, or apply it toward the entry fee for a future challenge. Any balance remaining in your wallet after a period of inactivity will be paid out to you automatically. An entry fee paid from your wallet balance is subject to the same terms as any other entry fee.

Free challenges: Some challenges have no entry fee and still award cash prizes. Free challenges are governed by these Terms in the same way as paid challenges, except that the entry-fee, payment, and refund provisions do not apply. No prize in a free challenge is allocated by chance.

Referral Program and Free Entries: Each account is issued a unique referral code. A referral is successful when a new user enters your code at sign-up and subsequently completes their first paid challenge — meaning a challenge with an entry fee that the user paid themselves and that runs to settlement. Entries funded by wallet balance or by a Free Entry do not qualify as a paid challenge for this purpose. When a referral is successful, referral credit is granted to both the referring user and the referred user.

Upon an account accruing five (5) referral credits, the account is granted one Free Entry: entry into a single paid challenge of the user's choosing with the entry fee covered by the Platform. A Free Entry is granted at most once per account, regardless of further referral credits. A user who joins a challenge using a Free Entry competes on the same terms as paying entrants, including prize eligibility; the challenge remains a paid challenge, and only that user's entry fee is covered. A Free Entry spent on a challenge is not restored if the user exits that challenge.

A Free Entry has no cash value, cannot be transferred, sold, or exchanged, and cannot be applied retroactively or combined with any other payment method. Referral credit and Free Entries may be revoked, and accounts may be suspended or terminated as described in Section 11, where we determine referrals were obtained through fraud or abuse, including self-referrals, referrals of duplicate or fabricated accounts, or any coordinated scheme to generate referrals. 6. Exiting a Challenge

You may voluntarily exit a challenge at any time before or after it begins. The following rules apply:

  • Before the challenge starts: You will be removed from the challenge, your entry-fee authorization is released, and you are not charged. You are free to join another challenge immediately.

7. Step Data Sync Requirements

To ensure accurate leaderboard standings and final results, users are responsible for the following:

  • A connected step source is required. The App reads your step data from Apple Health (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android). You are responsible for ensuring a step source is actively recording steps into your health app and that the App has been granted permission to read it. On Android in particular, Health Connect does not record steps on its own — it only stores step data provided by another app or a connected wearable. If no step source is feeding your health app, or if permission is not granted, your steps will register as zero regardless of your physical activity. It is your sole responsibility to confirm your steps are being recorded before and during a challenge.
  • Background sync permission is required to enroll. To enroll in a challenge, you must grant the App permission to read your step data while the App is in the background or closed, when prompted during enrollment (on iOS, through the Apple Health permissions screen; on Android, through the Health Connect background-access permission). This allows the App to keep your counted step total reasonably current on the challenge leaderboard without requiring you to open the App. Where your device reports the status of this permission, the App may decline enrollment while it is not granted. You agree to keep this permission enabled for the duration of any challenge you enter and its post-challenge sync window. Background syncing is best-efforts and controlled by your device's operating system; it supplements, and does not replace, your responsibilities under this Section. Sync recency is visible to other entrants. The time your steps were last synced to the Platform is displayed with your entry on the live leaderboard of any challenge you are enrolled in.
  • A single step source is used, and only that source counts. Before enrolling, one step source is set for your account — Apple Health by default on iOS, and Garmin on Android, which requires a connected Garmin device — and you may change it in Settings at any time before you enroll. Only steps recorded by that source will be counted toward your challenge results; steps from any other app, device, or source will not be included. The source is locked to your entry at the time you enroll and cannot be changed for the duration of that challenge. It is your responsibility to choose the source that accurately reflects the steps you physically take and to keep that source recording throughout the challenge. If your selected source stops recording or syncing, your step total may stop advancing, and the most recently synced total will be used. Manually entered steps are never counted, regardless of source. On iOS, eligible sources are Apple Health (covering your iPhone's built-in step counter and Apple Watch), Oura, and Garmin. On Android, Garmin is currently the only eligible source — if you use an Android device without a connected Garmin, no eligible step source is available to you and you will not be able to accumulate countable steps. Eligible sources may change as devices and platforms change.
  • Keep your apps synced during a challenge. During an active challenge, make sure your steps and any connected health or fitness apps (such as your wearable's companion app, for example Oura or Garmin Connect) are synced and up to date, and open the App regularly during the challenge. The App reads your step data from Apple Health or Google Health Connect when you open the App, and those services only contain your latest steps if your phone and any connected apps have synced.
  • Post-challenge sync window. After a challenge has ended, make sure your steps and any connected health or fitness apps are synced and up to date, and open the App within 24 hours of the challenge end time, so your complete step data is captured before results are finalized.
  • Settlement timing. Challenge results are finalized approximately 24 hours after the challenge end time. Step data not synced by settlement will not be included in your final result.
  • Incomplete sync liability. If you fail to sync within the 24-hour post-challenge window, your recorded step count at the time of settlement will be used as your final total. The Platform is not responsible for any difference between your actual steps and your synced step count.
  • Syncing depends on your apps and on opening our App. The App syncs your steps when you open it, and it can only read steps that your phone and connected apps have already synced into Apple Health or Google Health Connect. When you have granted background access, the App attempts to sync your steps periodically while it is closed or in the background, but background syncing is controlled by your device's operating system, is not guaranteed to run at any particular time or frequency, and may be prevented by device settings, battery optimization, or platform restrictions. You should not rely on background syncing alone. Keeping your health and fitness apps synced and up to date, and opening the App to sync — including within the post-challenge window described above — is your responsibility.
  • Android users: refresh Garmin Connect at the end of each day. On Android, Moneystep reads your steps from Google Health Connect, and Health Connect holds only what the Garmin Connect app has written into it. Steps that remain on your watch and have not synced to Garmin Connect do not exist as far as the App is concerned. Refresh Garmin Connect at the end of each day, after you have taken your steps for that day. Refreshing earlier in the day does not reliably write that day's full step count into Health Connect, and steps taken after an early refresh may not be written for that day at all. This is a limitation of how Garmin Connect and Health Connect exchange data and is outside the Platform's control. We also recommend opening Moneystep once a day so you can confirm your total is advancing as expected. If your recorded total is lower than the steps you physically took because your source did not sync, the Platform is not responsible for the difference.
  • Android battery optimization. Some Android device manufacturers (including but not limited to Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, and OnePlus) apply aggressive battery-saving features that can prevent the App from receiving notifications and can interfere with syncing between Garmin Connect, Health Connect, and the App. If you use one of these devices, we recommend exempting both Moneystep and Garmin Connect from battery optimization in your device settings. You can typically find this under Battery → App optimization or Battery → Background usage limits. The Platform is not responsible for missed notifications or incomplete step data resulting from manufacturer battery-optimization settings.
  • No liability for device or connectivity issues. The Platform is not responsible for incomplete, missing, or delayed step data resulting from device settings, device failures, manufacturer restrictions, network outages, or failure to sync within required timeframes.
  • If you never open the App, your steps cannot be recorded. If you do not open the App at all during a challenge and also do not open it within 24 hours after the challenge ends, no step data can be captured for that challenge. In that case your steps cannot be recorded and your final total for the challenge will be zero (0), regardless of how many steps you physically took.
  • Disputing your final result. It is your responsibility to raise any dispute about your own result in a settled challenge in time. If you believe your final step count or placement is incorrect — for example, steps you physically took were not counted — you must notify us at hello@moneystep.app within seven (7) days after that challenge is settled. Your notice must identify the challenge and describe the claimed error. After that seven-day window, your final result is deemed accepted and final as to you and is no longer subject to dispute or adjustment on your request. Whether and how to act on any notice is within the Platform's sole discretion, and nothing in this section obligates the Platform to change any result or to act within any particular timeframe.
