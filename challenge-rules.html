Challenge Rules

How Challenges Work

1. These Rules and How They Apply

These Official Challenge Rules (the “Rules”) govern every step-count competition (each, a “Challenge”) offered through the Moneystep mobile application (the “App”), which is operated by Moneystep LLC (the “Platform”). By enrolling in a Challenge, you agree to these Rules, to the Moneystep Terms of Service, and to the Moneystep Privacy Policy. If these Rules conflict with the Terms of Service with respect to the conduct of a Challenge, these Rules control for that Challenge; the Terms of Service otherwise remain in full effect.

Each Challenge also has Challenge-specific details posted in the App at the time of enrollment — including its start and end times, enrollment window, entry fee, prize schedule, minimum number of entrants, and any rank requirement. Those posted details form part of these Rules for that Challenge. The version of these Rules in effect at the time you enroll in a Challenge governs that Challenge for you.

2. Skill-Based Competition

Every Challenge is a skill-based endurance competition. Outcomes are determined entirely by the number of steps each entrant physically takes during the Challenge, as recorded by that entrant’s approved step source — a result of physical effort, fitness, and sustained activity that is wholly within each entrant’s own control. No element of chance, drawing, or random selection determines any outcome, and no prize is awarded on the basis of anything other than counted steps. Challenges are not gambling and are not games of chance.

3. Eligibility

To enroll in a Challenge, you must satisfy all of the following at the time of enrollment:

  • Account: you have a Moneystep account in good standing. You may maintain only one account, and only you may use it.
  • Age: you are at least 18 years of age. You must complete age and identity verification before your first withdrawal.
  • Location: you are a resident of the United States and are physically located in an Eligible State (Section 4) at the time of account creation, at the time of enrollment, and while taking any steps counted toward a Challenge.
  • Not excluded: you are not an Excluded Person (Section 5).
  • Not self-excluded: you are not currently self-excluded. You may exclude yourself from paid Challenges at any time from the App’s settings, for 30 days, 90 days, one year, or permanently; a self-exclusion cannot be shortened or reversed before the period you selected has ended.
  • Step history: your account has recorded step data from an approved step source over the preceding four weeks, sufficient to show that your source is active and reliably recording. Manually entered step data does not count toward this requirement. The Platform may adjust the parameters of this requirement at its discretion.
  • Background sync permission: you have granted the App background access to your step data as described in Section 11, and you keep it enabled throughout the Challenge and its post-Challenge sync window.
  • Rank: where a Challenge carries a rank requirement, your assigned rank tier — based on your recent step activity and recalculated regularly — meets that requirement. A change in your rank during a Challenge does not affect that Challenge.
  • One Challenge at a time: you are not enrolled in any other active Challenge. You may participate in only one active Challenge at a time.

Location verification: The Platform verifies your location at account creation, at each paid challenge entry, and at each withdrawal request, using a third-party provider designed to detect VPN use and location spoofing. It remains your sole responsibility to ensure that your participation is lawful in your jurisdiction at all times. The Platform may verify eligibility at any time and may disqualify entries and withhold prizes where participation violates these Rules or applicable law.

4. Eligible States

Challenges are open only to entrants physically located in the following states (each an “Eligible State”): Colorado, Illinois, Minnesota, New York, North Carolina, and Texas.

Participation from any other U.S. state, territory, or from outside the United States is not permitted. The list of Eligible States may change over time; the list in effect at the time of your enrollment, as reflected in the App, governs that Challenge.

5. Excluded Persons

The following persons (“Excluded Persons”) are not eligible to enroll in any Challenge that has an entry fee or awards a cash prize: employees, owners, members, officers, directors, agents, and contractors of the Platform, and members of their households.

Excluded Persons may participate only in Challenges that award no cash prize. If an Excluded Person is found to have enrolled in a paid or cash-prize Challenge, the Platform may remove the entry at any time, and any prize position that entry would have occupied passes to the next eligible entrant in the standings.

6. Entry and Entry Fees

To enter a Challenge, enroll in the App during that Challenge’s enrollment window and pay its entry fee, if any. The entry fee for each Challenge is a single flat amount disclosed in the App before you enroll; every entrant in a Challenge pays the same fee. Entry fees do not exceed $40 per Challenge.

Payment. Entry fees are collected through a licensed third-party payment processor. When you enroll, the processor places an authorization on your payment method; funds are captured when the Challenge starts. If the Challenge does not start, the authorization is released and you are not charged.

Paying from your wallet. If you hold a wallet balance from prior winnings, you may apply it to an entry fee instead of paying externally. A wallet-paid entry fee is subject to the same rules — including the refund rules in Section 15 — as any other entry fee.

Free Entries. If you have been granted a Free Entry through the referral program described in the Terms of Service, you may apply it to a single paid Challenge of your choosing. The Challenge remains a paid Challenge and you compete on the same terms as paying entrants, including prize eligibility; only your entry fee is covered. A Free Entry spent on a Challenge is not restored if you exit that Challenge.

Free Challenges. Some Challenges have no entry fee and may still award cash prizes. Free Challenges are governed by these Rules in the same way as paid Challenges, except that the entry-fee and refund provisions do not apply. No prize in a Free Challenge is allocated by chance.

No entry may be submitted on behalf of another person, and payment must be made using a payment method you own or are authorized to use.

7. Challenge Format and Prize Schedules

Each Challenge’s prize schedule — the number of winning placements and the exact dollar amount for each placement — is fixed and is posted in the App before enrollment opens. Prize amounts do not depend on, and do not scale with, the number of entrants or the amount of entry fees collected.

The prize guarantee and its single condition: Every Challenge states a minimum number of entrants before enrollment opens. That minimum is the only condition on the prize. If the minimum is not met by the enrollment deadline, the Challenge does not proceed: it is cancelled automatically, no funds are captured, every entry fee already collected is refunded in full, no result is recorded, and no prize is awarded.

If the minimum is met, the Challenge proceeds and the posted prizes become an unconditional obligation of the Platform. They are paid in full, exactly as posted, regardless of how many entrants above the minimum enroll, regardless of how much is collected in entry fees, and regardless of whether entry fees cover the prizes. The Platform bears any shortfall. No prize amount is reduced, withheld, or recalculated because enrollment was lower than expected.

Prizes are funded by the Platform, or by a sponsor identified for the Challenge, from a segregated reserve funded before enrollment opens. The Platform takes no percentage of, and no fee from, any prize.

Entrants who finish outside the cash-winning placements receive points, as described in the Terms of Service. Points are not a prize, have no cash value, and are not allocated by chance.

8. Scoring

Your score in a Challenge is the total number of steps you physically take between the Challenge’s start time and end time, as automatically recorded by your locked step source (Sections 9 and 10) and as synced to the Platform by settlement (Section 11). Standings are ordered by total counted steps, highest first.

  • Only steps automatically recorded by your locked source count. Steps recorded by any other app, device, or source do not count, even if they appear in your health app.
  • Manually entered step data never counts, from any source, under any circumstances. Any attempt to submit manually entered data is treated as an attempt to manipulate Challenge results (Section 17).
  • Steps taken outside the Challenge’s start and end times do not count.

The Platform’s records at settlement are the scoring records for a Challenge, subject only to Sections 18–20.

9. Approved Step Sources

The App reads step data through Apple Health (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android), and counts steps only from approved sources. The approved sources are currently:

  • On iPhone: Apple Health (covering the iPhone’s built-in step counter and Apple Watch), Garmin, and Oura Ring.
  • On Android: Garmin only. If you use an Android device without a connected Garmin, no approved step source is available to you and you will not be able to accumulate countable steps.

Approved sources are vetted and determined by the Platform in its discretion and may change as devices and platforms change; the App reflects the sources available to your account.

10. The Source Lock

One step source is set for your account before you enroll, and you may change it in the App’s settings at any time before enrolling. When you enroll in a Challenge, your then-selected source is locked to that entry and cannot be changed for the duration of that Challenge, including its post-Challenge sync window. Only steps recorded by your locked source count toward that Challenge.

It is your responsibility to choose the source that accurately reflects the steps you physically take, and to keep that source recording and syncing throughout the Challenge. If your locked source stops recording or syncing, your counted total may stop advancing, and your most recently synced total will be used.

11. Syncing and the Post-Challenge Sync Window

The App reads your step data when you open it, and it can only read steps that your phone and connected apps have already delivered into Apple Health or Google Health Connect. Keeping your data synced is your responsibility:

  • During a Challenge: keep your wearable and its companion app (for example, Garmin Connect or the Oura app) synced and up to date, and open the App regularly.
  • Battery optimization: some Android manufacturers apply aggressive battery-saving features that can interfere with syncing between Garmin Connect, Health Connect, and the App. We recommend exempting both Moneystep and Garmin Connect from battery optimization in your device settings.
  • Post-Challenge sync window: after a Challenge ends, sync your apps and open the App within 24 hours of the Challenge end time, so your complete step data is captured before results are finalized.
  • Settlement: results are finalized (“settled”) approximately 24 hours after the Challenge end time. Step data not synced to the Platform by settlement is not included in your final total; your most recently synced total is used.

Android users — refresh Garmin Connect at the end of each day: On Android, Health Connect holds only what Garmin Connect has written into it. Steps that remain on your watch and have not synced to Garmin Connect do not exist as far as the App is concerned. Refresh Garmin Connect at the end of each day, after you have taken your steps for that day. Refreshing earlier in the day does not reliably write that day’s full step count into Health Connect, and steps taken after an early refresh may not be written for that day at all. This is a limitation of how Garmin Connect and Health Connect exchange data and is outside the Platform’s control.

If you never open the App, your total is zero: If you never open the App during a Challenge or within its post-Challenge sync window, no step data can be captured and your final total will be zero, regardless of how many steps you physically took.

Background syncing supplements, and does not replace, your sync responsibilities. When you have granted background access, the App attempts to read and sync your steps periodically while closed or in the background. Background syncing is controlled by your device’s operating system and is not guaranteed to run at any particular time or frequency. Your obligations in this Section apply regardless of background syncing.

Sync recency is visible. Each entrant’s leaderboard entry in a live Challenge displays the time that entrant’s steps were last synced. A total that has not synced recently may be materially lower than the steps that entrant has physically taken.

The Platform is not responsible for incomplete, missing, or delayed step data resulting from device settings, device or app failures, manufacturer battery-optimization restrictions, network outages, or failure to sync within the required timeframes.

12. Winner Determination and Settlement

At settlement, entrants are ranked by total counted steps, highest first, and the posted prize schedule is applied to the final standings. Prize placements are awarded to the highest-ranked eligible entrants; any placement that would fall to an ineligible or disqualified entry passes to the next eligible entrant in the standings. Prizes are credited to winners’ in-app wallets at settlement, subject to the integrity, verification, and eligibility provisions of Section 3 and Sections 17–20.

13. Ties

If two or more entrants tie for a cash-winning placement, they split the combined prize amounts of the placements occupied by the tied entrants. For example, if three entrants tie for 4th place in a Challenge with ten cash-winning places, those three split the combined 4th, 5th, and 6th place prizes, and the entrant with the next-highest step count receives the 7th place prize. If entrants tie for the final cash-winning place, they split that single prize.

Each tied entrant’s share is computed in whole cents, rounded down to the nearest cent, with any remainder retained by the Platform. Every entrant occupying a cash-winning placement receives a payment of at least one cent — no cash placement is ever paid zero.

Because ties can affect the amount an individual entrant receives, posted per-placement amounts are shown as expected amounts until the Challenge is settled. The total posted prize for the Challenge does not change.

14. Cancellation

Before a Challenge starts. A Challenge that does not reach its posted minimum number of entrants by its enrollment deadline is cancelled and does not proceed. The Platform may also cancel a Challenge before it starts for any other reason. If a Challenge is cancelled before it starts, no funds are captured, all entry fees received for it are refunded in full — fees paid externally are returned, fees paid from wallet balance are restored to the wallet, and a Free Entry applied to that Challenge is restored — and enrollees are notified.

After a Challenge starts. The Platform may cancel a Challenge after it has begun only where the Challenge cannot be completed fairly or lawfully, including a technical failure affecting the integrity of scoring, a legal or regulatory requirement, or an event beyond the Platform’s reasonable control. In that case no results are recorded for that Challenge, no prizes are awarded, no result is entered in any entrant’s career statistics, and every entry fee for that Challenge is refunded in full.

15. Refunds

Entry fees are final: Except as provided in Section 14 (cancellation by the Platform), all entry fees are final and non-refundable in all circumstances — including voluntary exit before or after the Challenge begins, injury, illness, device malfunction, loss of connectivity, step-sync failure, removal or disqualification for prohibited conduct, and dissatisfaction with results.

If you exit before a Challenge starts, your entry-fee authorization is released and you are not charged.

16. Exiting a Challenge

You may voluntarily exit a Challenge at any time, before or after it starts.

  • Before the Challenge starts: your authorization is released and you are not charged. You are free to enroll in another Challenge immediately.
  • After the Challenge starts: your entry fee is not refunded, and a Free Entry applied to that Challenge is not restored. You are removed from the Challenge and its leaderboard, you receive no prize, and you are free to enroll in another Challenge immediately.

Exits are recorded in your career statistics; exiting after a Challenge has started counts as a last-place finish for statistical purposes. Steps taken during an exited Challenge are not credited toward your career step totals or any global leaderboard.

17. Prohibited Conduct and Disqualification

The following conduct is prohibited in connection with any Challenge. The full prohibited-conduct provisions of the Terms of Service (§11) also apply. Prohibited conduct includes, without limitation:

  • Manually entering, editing, importing, or otherwise inputting step or health data by hand rather than physically taking the steps.
  • Using any device, application, or method to artificially generate or inflate step counts, or any other manipulation of step or health data.
  • Creating or using multiple accounts, participating under a false identity, or using or sharing another person’s account.
  • Generating or attempting to generate referral credit through fraud or abuse, including self-referrals, referrals of duplicate or fabricated accounts, or any coordinated scheme to accumulate referrals.
  • Colluding, coordinating, or agreeing with other entrants to manipulate outcomes or share winnings.
  • Exploiting bugs, vulnerabilities, or malfunctions in the App, or using bots, scripts, or other automation to interact with it.
  • Using a VPN, proxy, location-spoofing tool, or any other method to disguise or misrepresent your location.
  • Participating while physically located outside an Eligible State, or while otherwise ineligible under Section 3.
  • Submitting payment with a method you do not own or are not authorized to use.

Where the Platform determines that an entrant has engaged in prohibited conduct, it may disqualify the entry, remove the entrant from the Challenge without refund, withhold or reclaim any associated prize, forfeit associated winnings and wallet balance in accordance with the Terms of Service, and suspend or permanently ban the account. A prize position vacated by a disqualified entry passes to the next eligible entrant in the standings. Determinations are made through the process described in Sections 18 and 19.

18. Integrity Reviews and Verification

The Platform monitors Challenge activity for signs of manipulation, irregularity, or conduct inconsistent with these Rules.

The Platform may at any time require you to verify your identity, your activity, or your step data — including where activity associated with your account is identified as suspicious, irregular, or anomalous. If verification is requested:

  • You must respond and provide the requested information within seven (7) days of the request. We will send at least two notices during that period.
  • While a review is ongoing, any prize, wallet balance, or withdrawal associated with the Challenge or activity under review is placed on hold. A hold is not a forfeiture: held amounts are released if the review resolves in your favor. Funds in your account that are not associated with the activity under review are not held.
  • At the conclusion of a review the Platform will provide a written determination stating the basis for its decision. Forfeiture occurs only where the Platform determines that a violation occurred.
  • If you fail to respond within the seven-day period, or provide false or materially incomplete information, the Platform may make a determination on the information available to it, and the associated entry may be disqualified and the associated amounts forfeited, and your account suspended or banned — subject to your appeal right in Section 19.

The Platform determines in its discretion what constitutes an adequate response, and is not obligated to release held amounts while a review is ongoing.

Repeated suspicious activity. Where activity associated with your account is repeatedly identified as suspicious, the Platform may suspend or permanently terminate your account and forfeit any wallet balance and pending winnings, whether or not any individual instance was separately confirmed as a violation and whether or not you were notified of any prior instance. The methods, criteria, and thresholds the Platform uses to identify suspicious activity are confidential and are not disclosed.

19. Appeals

You may appeal a disqualification, forfeiture, or other adverse integrity determination one time. To appeal, email hello@moneystep.app within seven (7) days of the notice of the determination, identifying the Challenge and stating the grounds for your appeal and any supporting information.

An appeal is reviewed by a person other than the person who made the original determination, and the Platform will respond within ten (10) business days. Amounts on hold remain on hold during the appeal. The appeal decision is final. A determination that is not appealed within the seven-day window becomes final. You must complete this appeal process before commencing arbitration under Section 16 of the Terms of Service in respect of that determination.

20. Disputing Your Own Result

Separately from integrity appeals, if you believe your own final step count or placement in a settled Challenge is incorrect — for example, steps you physically took were not counted — you must notify the Platform at hello@moneystep.app within seven (7) days after that Challenge is settled, identifying the Challenge and describing the claimed error. After that window, your final result is deemed accepted and final as to you. Whether and how to act on any notice is within the Platform’s discretion.

21. Prize Payment and Taxes

Prizes are credited to your in-app wallet at settlement and are paid through a licensed third-party payment processor. You may request a withdrawal from the Payments & Wallet section of the App’s settings. Your location is verified and your age and identity verification must be complete before your first withdrawal is processed.

You are solely responsible for the accuracy of your withdrawal information; the Platform is not responsible for payments misdirected due to information you provided. Wallet balances may alternatively be applied to future entry fees. Any balance remaining in your wallet after a period of inactivity is paid out to you automatically.

You are solely responsible for all taxes on prizes. If your cumulative gross winnings in a calendar year equal or exceed $2,000, you will be required to provide a valid Social Security Number or Employer Identification Number before further payouts are processed, and the Platform will issue a Form 1099 as required by law. Failure to provide required tax information may result in a hold on winnings until it is received.
